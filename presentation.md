## Cybersecurity & Crime

This page contains the presentation slides and English references for my **BİL 333 (Mesleki Yabancı Dil-1)** academic presentation on **Cybersecurity & Crime**.

### Overview
My presentation explores how cybercrime works, how big the threat has become according to 2025–2026 global data, and how both organizations and individuals can defend themselves using modern cybersecurity frameworks and daily habits.
